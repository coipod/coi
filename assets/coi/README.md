The supplied design was used to generate a complete-pose atlas; that atlas was used as reference for the SD master, and complete individual SD images were then generated from the master. No independently generated anatomical parts are assembled at runtime. Generation prompts and asset hashes are recorded in [sd-generation.json](sd-generation.json); runtime relationships are in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Opaque atlas backgrounds and bounded PixiJS deformation are intentional limitations, not a layered Cubism rig.
