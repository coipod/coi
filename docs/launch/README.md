## Positioning

EN: An open-source coding workspace with a character companion.

JA: キャラクターと一緒に開発できる、オープンソースのコーディングワークスペース。

Show the character first, then a real request, provider response and reviewed change. Never claim offline AI, free provider usage, general Windows support or Cubism Live2D. Source builds and exact CLI compatibility gates are material limitations, not footnotes to hide.

## Operating rhythm

Publish one English and one Japanese short post each week from one maker account. Spend 90 minutes preparing content, 60 minutes on relevant conversations, 60 minutes on feedback and 30 minutes on measurement. Use the remaining hour for revisions. Choose posting times when the maker can respond; review replies within the next day. No purchased stars, exchanges, bulk DMs, repetitive submissions or app telemetry.

D0 is assigned only after public-release approval and gates are met. Dates stay relative until then. A missed target triggers a channel/onboarding review, not additional duplicate posts. This kit is AI-assisted draft material: the maker must check factual claims and voice before posting. HN text must be written independently by the maker under its current rules.
