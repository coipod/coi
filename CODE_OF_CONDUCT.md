# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome contributors regardless of identity, experience or background. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions and restrict participation, with an explanation and a chance to appeal where practical. Report conduct concerns privately to [coipoddev@gmail.com](mailto:coipoddev@gmail.com) in English or Japanese. Include relevant context and links where appropriate; do not post personal information in a public issue.

The owner designated this address for publication on 2026-09-22. The repository remains private during release preparation; see docs/release/READINESS.md for remaining conditions. No response-time SLA is promised.
