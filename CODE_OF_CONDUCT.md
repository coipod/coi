# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome contributors regardless of identity, experience or background. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions and restrict participation, with an explanation and a chance to appeal where practical. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the published private contact channel; do not post personal information in a public issue. A contact must be configured before community launch.

During private preparation at https://github.com/coipod/coi, community launch is blocked until the owner supplies a private operating/conduct contact approved for publication. No email address has been inferred from Git commit metadata. See docs/release/READINESS.md; never use a public issue for a private conduct report.
